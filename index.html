5. Are Scientific Farming Methods Effective to Solve Farming Problems in China?
5.1. What are Scientific Farming Methods?

Scientific farming methods refer to the use of technology in farming.

2 Major goals: Solve farming problems and increase farming output/ farming productivity.

5 common scientific farming methods

  1. Irrigation systems
  2. Using chemicals
  3. Machines
  4. Greenhouses
  5. Genetically modified crops (GM Crops)
5.2. Using Irrigation Systems

Sprinklers

  • Irrigation water from surface water (e.g. reservoirs and rivers) and groundwater.

Advantages:

  • Increase water supply in dry areas.
  • Increase farming area.
  • Lengthen growing season.
  • Higher crop yields/crop production

Pesticide Herbicide/Weedicide Chemical Fertilizers

• Reduces the risk of crop failure.
• Increases crop yields. 		• Controls weeds.

• Disadvantages of overusing pesticide/herbicide:

1. Amount of pests may increase in the long run. Pesticides will kill all insects/kill good insects.
Pests may develop resistance to pesticides.
2. Death of living things in the water. Pesticides/Herbicides washed into the rivers will cause water pollution.
3. Harmful to people's health. When people eat the contaminated farm produce, it would be harmful to people's health.

Disadvantages of overusing chemical fertilizers:

When the chemical fertilizers are washed into rivers, or down into groundwater
1. Pollution (Soil & Water) Causing water pollution.
Chemical fertilizers may change the acidity of the soil ⇒ Soil pollution.
2. Death of living things in water Fertilizers are nutrients for algae. When fertilizers are washed into rivers, algae grow rapidly. Causing eutrophication. And algae use up oxygen in the river. Death of living things in the water.
3. Harmful to people's health. When people consume farm produce with high levels of chemicals, It would be harmful to their health.
4. Cause global warming Overusing chemical fertilizers may release large amount of greenhouse gases (nitrous oxide). Intensify greenhouse effect. Causes global warming.

5.7 Limitations of using scientific farming methods

If scientific farming methods are so useful in solving farming problems in China, why does not every farmer use them?

Requirement of the methods Farmers in China
High capital (High cost) Some farmers are too poor to afford the high costs of installing such farming technologies.
High technological know-how Some farmers have low education level so they do not know how to use such methods to farm.
